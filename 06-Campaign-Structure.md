## 6. Ads Structure

Each ad set contains the same four creative concepts.

### AD1 – Problem/Pain

Objective:
Highlight the difficulty of finding suitable industrial property.

### AD2 – Property/Inventory

Objective:
Show industrial property categories such as factories, warehouses, industrial plots and industrial buildings.

### AD3 – Trust/Experience

Objective:
Build credibility and reduce buyer hesitation.

### AD4 – Offer/CTA

Objective:
Encourage users to share their property requirements and generate leads.

## 7. Total Ads

3 Ad Sets × 4 Ads = 12 Ads

There are four unique creative concepts, replicated across the three audience segments.

## 11. Campaign Flow

User sees Facebook/Instagram advertisement.

↓

User clicks or interacts with the advertisement.

↓

User opens Instant Form.

↓

User submits contact and property requirement details.

↓

Lead is generated.

↓

Lead can be contacted for further qualification.

## 12. Campaign Testing Framework

The campaign compares three audience hypotheses while keeping four creative concepts consistent.

This helps identify:

- Best audience
- Best creative
- Best CPL
- Best qualified lead rate
- Best potential conversion opportunity
