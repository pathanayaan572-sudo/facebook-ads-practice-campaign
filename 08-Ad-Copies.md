# Common Messaging

## Primary Message
Explore industrial property options in Noida according to your business or investment requirements.

## Property Categories
- Factory
- Warehouse
- Industrial Plot
- Industrial Building
- Industrial Shed
- Commercial Industrial Unit

## Target Action
Submit an enquiry through the Instant Lead Form.

## Important Advertising Rule
Do not use unverified:

- Property prices
- Inventory numbers
- Guaranteed returns
- Appreciation claims
- False urgency
- Guaranteed investment outcomes

All property-specific claims should be verified before being used in a live campaign.
