## 5. Market Demand

## 6. Key Demand Drivers

### Business Growth
Expansion of businesses can create demand for factories, warehouses and industrial spaces.

### Connectivity
Noida has connectivity with Delhi and other parts of the NCR, supporting commercial and industrial activity.

### Manufacturing
Manufacturing businesses require suitable industrial spaces for operations, storage and production.

### Logistics
The growth of logistics and warehousing activities can create demand for industrial properties.

### Infrastructure Development
Continued infrastructure development can support long-term commercial and industrial real estate demand.

## 9. Market Challenges

### High Property Values
Industrial properties can require significant capital investment.

### Buyer Decision Time
Industrial property purchases are generally high-value decisions and may have a longer consideration period.

### Location Sensitivity
Industrial buyers may have specific requirements regarding sector, connectivity and surrounding infrastructure.

### Property Verification
Buyers may require verification of ownership, approvals, usage permissions and other documentation before making a decision.
