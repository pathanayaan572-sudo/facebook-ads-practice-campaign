# Audience Research – Noida Industrial Properties

## 1. Primary Target Audience

The primary audience consists of people who may have a business, operational or investment requirement for industrial property in Noida.

## 2. Geographic Targeting

Primary Location:
- Noida, Uttar Pradesh

Potential secondary markets for future testing:
- Greater Noida
- Delhi
- Ghaziabad
- Gurugram
- Faridabad

For the initial practice campaign, the primary location is Noida.

## 3. Age

Primary testing range:
30–60+

Reason:
Industrial property is generally a high-value purchase and may attract established business owners, entrepreneurs and investors.

## 4. Gender

All genders.

Reason:
Industrial property requirements are not restricted to a specific gender.

## 5. Audience Segment 1 – Broad

### Targeting
- Location: Noida
- Age: 30–60+
- Gender: All
- Detailed Targeting: None

### Hypothesis
Meta's algorithm may identify potential industrial-property leads without additional interest restrictions.

## 6. Audience Segment 2 – Business & Industrial

### Targeting
Relevant interests may include:

- Entrepreneurship
- Small Business
- Manufacturing
- Logistics
- Warehousing
- Supply Chain
- Commercial Real Estate
- Real Estate

### Hypothesis
People interested in business and industrial activities may have a higher probability of requiring industrial property.

## 7. Audience Segment 3 – Real Estate Investor

### Targeting
Relevant interests may include:

- Real Estate
- Commercial Real Estate
- Real Estate Investing
- Investment
- Entrepreneurship

### Hypothesis
People interested in real estate and investment may have an interest in purchasing industrial property for investment or business purposes.

## 11. Audience Testing Strategy

The campaign will compare:

1. Broad Audience
2. Business & Industrial Audience
3. Real Estate Investor Audience

The audiences will be evaluated using:

- Leads
- Cost per Lead
- CTR
- CPC
- Qualified Leads
- Lead Quality
- Site Visits
- Conversion potential

## 12. Targeting Principle

Detailed targeting will not be used simply because an option is available in Meta Ads Manager.

Each targeting signal should have a logical relationship with the potential buyer.

Interest:
Used when the signal represents a relevant interest or topic.

Demographic:
Used when a relevant demographic characteristic is available and supported by the audience hypothesis.

Behavior:
Used only when a relevant behavior has a logical connection to the campaign objective.

## 13. Audience Research Conclusion

The campaign uses three audience hypotheses to identify which type of audience can generate the most relevant industrial-property enquiries.

The Broad audience tests Meta's optimization capability, while Business & Industrial and Real Estate Investor audiences test interest-based hypotheses.
