# Creative Strategy – Noida Industrial Properties

## 1. Creative Objective

The creatives are designed to attract potential buyers and investors interested in industrial properties in Noida and encourage them to submit a lead form.

## 2. Creative Formats

The campaign can use:

- Short-form vertical videos
- Static images
- Carousel ads

Primary format:
Short-form video

Recommended ratio:
9:16

## 3. AD1 – Problem/Pain Angle

### Objective
Capture attention by highlighting the difficulty of finding a suitable industrial property.

### Hook
"Noida mein industrial property dhoondh rahe hain, lekin right property mil nahi rahi?"

### Visual Concept
- Noida industrial area
- Factory exterior
- Warehouse
- Industrial property search
- Business owner reviewing property options

### On-Screen Text
"Noida Industrial Property"
"Factory | Warehouse | Plot | Building"

### Message
Finding a suitable industrial property requires consideration of location, size, property type and business requirements.

### CTA
Get Property Details

## 5. AD3 – Trust/Experience Angle

### Objective
Build credibility and reduce hesitation for a high-value property decision.

### Hook
"Industrial property lene se pehle sirf property nahi, right guidance bhi zaroori hai."

### Visual Concept
- Property consultant
- Industrial property footage
- Site visit
- Client consultation
- Property Mart branding

### On-Screen Text
"Experience-Based Property Assistance"
"Industrial Property Expertise"

### Message
Industrial property is a significant business and investment decision. Buyers may require assistance in understanding location, property type and suitability.

### CTA

### Note
Any company experience figures or claims should be used only after verification from the business.

## 7. Creative Testing Strategy

The same four creative concepts will be used across all three ad sets.

AD1:
Problem/Pain

AD2:
Property/Inventory

AD3:
Trust/Experience

AD4:
Offer/CTA

This keeps the creative variables consistent while testing different audience segments.

## 8. Creative KPIs

The creatives will be evaluated using:

- Impressions
- Reach
- CPM
- CTR
- CPC
- Landing Page Views
- Leads
- CPL
- Qualified Leads
- Cost per Qualified Lead

## 9. Creative Optimization

If a creative has low CTR:
Test a new hook or visual.

If CTR is strong but leads are low:
Review the offer, landing page or lead form.

If CPL is low but lead quality is poor:
Review targeting, messaging and lead qualification.

If a creative consistently generates qualified leads:
Consider increasing its delivery through budget allocation and scaling.

## 10. Creative Compliance

The campaign should avoid:

- Unverified property prices
- Fake inventory numbers
- Guaranteed returns
- Guaranteed appreciation
- Misleading claims
- False urgency

All property-specific claims should be verified before being used in a live campaign.

## 11. Creative Strategy Conclusion

Four different creative angles are used to test different stages of the buyer decision process:

Problem → Product → Trust → Action

The goal is to identify which creative generates the most relevant industrial-property enquiries.
