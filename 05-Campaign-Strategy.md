# Campaign Strategy – Noida Industrial Properties

## 1. Campaign Objective

Primary Objective:
Lead Generation

The campaign is designed to generate enquiries from potential buyers and investors interested in Noida industrial properties.

## 2. Campaign Conversion

Conversion Location:
Instant Forms

Performance Goal:
Maximize number of leads

## 3. Campaign Strategy

The campaign will test three different audience hypotheses while keeping the creative concepts consistent.

This allows the campaign to compare audience performance more clearly.

## 4. Campaign Structure

One campaign will contain three ad sets.

### Ad Set 1
AS1_BROAD_NOIDA

Purpose:
Test Meta's ability to identify potential leads without detailed targeting.

### Ad Set 2
AS2_Business_Industrial

Purpose:
Target people with relevant business and industrial interests.

### Ad Set 3
AS3_RealEstate_Investor

Purpose:
Target people with relevant real estate and investment interests.

## 5. Creative Strategy

Four creative concepts will be used across all three ad sets.

### AD1 – Problem/Pain
Focus:
Identify the difficulty of finding suitable industrial property.

### AD2 – Property/Inventory
Focus:
Show the types of industrial properties available.

### AD3 – Trust/Experience
Focus:
Build credibility and reduce buyer hesitation.

### AD4 – Offer/CTA
Focus:
Encourage users to share their property requirement and generate a lead.

## 6. Testing Strategy

The campaign will use the same four creative concepts across the three audience segments.

This creates a controlled comparison between audience groups.

The campaign will evaluate:

- CTR
- CPC
- CPM
- Leads
- CPL
- Qualified Leads
- Lead Quality
- Site Visits
- Conversion Potential

## 8. Bid Strategy

Initial Bid Strategy:
Highest Volume / Maximize Results

The campaign will initially allow Meta to optimize for the maximum number of leads.

Manual bidding will not be used during the initial testing phase.

## 9. Placements

Initial placement strategy:
Advantage+ Placements

The campaign will allow Meta to distribute ads across eligible Facebook and Instagram placements.

## 10. Optimization Strategy

The campaign will be evaluated based on both lead quantity and lead quality.

If an audience generates a low CPL but poor-quality leads, it will not automatically be considered the winner.

The preferred audience should generate relevant and potentially qualified enquiries.

## 11. Scaling Strategy

If an audience consistently generates qualified leads at an acceptable CPL, its budget may be increased gradually.

Poor-performing audiences or creatives may have their budgets reduced or may be paused after sufficient data is available.

## 12. Retargeting Strategy

Retargeting can be introduced after sufficient traffic or engagement data is available.

Potential retargeting audiences may include:

- Website visitors
- Facebook/Instagram engagers
- Video viewers
- Lead form openers
- Lead form submitters

Retargeting is not part of the initial three-ad-set testing structure.

## 13. Campaign Hypothesis

The campaign hypothesis is that:

1. Broad targeting may allow Meta's algorithm to find relevant leads efficiently.
2. Business and industrial interests may provide stronger relevance among business-related prospects.
3. Real estate and investment interests may generate prospects interested in property ownership or investment.

## 14. Success Criteria

The campaign will not judge success using CPL alone.

Primary indicators:

- Qualified Lead Rate
- Cost per Qualified Lead
- Lead-to-Site-Visit Rate
- Potential Sales
- Cost per Acquisition

Secondary indicators:

- CTR
- CPC
- CPM
- Frequency
- Overall CPL

## 15. Campaign Strategy Conclusion

The campaign is structured as an audience-testing exercise.

Three audience approaches and four creative concepts will be used to identify the combination that can potentially generate the most relevant industrial-property enquiries.
